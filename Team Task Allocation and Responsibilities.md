## 4. Collaboration and Integration

All team members will contribute to a shared GitHub repository. Each member will manage their assigned artifacts through individual branches and commits. Pull Requests will be used to review and integrate contributions into the main branch.

The team will coordinate the development of written requirements and diagrams to ensure consistency across the project. All approved contributions will be integrated into the final SRS deliverable.
