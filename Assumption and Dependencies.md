# Assumptions and Dependencies

### Assumptions :

* Most QAU students and faculty own a smartphone with internet access .
* Hut management is willing to adopt th e system and train its staff to use it.
* The university will provide or allow the use of a cloud or universitymanaged server for hosting.
* The huts will hire or assign enough delivery staff to handle the order volum.
* Students in distant departments will prefer ordering through the app over walking to the huts.
* Hut admins will keep the menu and item availability up to date.

### Dependencies

**Payment Gateway APIs:**  
The system depends on Easypaisa and JazzCash providing working APIs for online payments and refunds.

**Push Notification Service:**  
The system depends on a service such as Fire base Cloud Messaging to send realtime alerts.

**SMS / Email Service:**  
OTP delivery depends on an email or SMS provider.

**Internet Infrastructure:**  
The system depends on a stable QAU campus network.

**University Support:**  
The system depends on QAU allowing the app to be promoted and officially used on campus.

Cash on Delivery is a payment option handled by delivery staff. It is not an API dependency.
