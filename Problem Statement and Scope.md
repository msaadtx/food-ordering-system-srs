**1.4 Problem statement:** 
Students at QAU face alot of difficulties when they try to get food from campus huts 
because the whole process is done manually and is quite outdated. There is no proper 
online platform where student can see the menu, place orders from somewhere else, 
pay digitally or get food delivered to them. 
Because of this students waste their time, feel uncomfortable, and many time they just 
skip food. huts also have problem of bad management and slow service because there 
is no digital system to help them. 
So we clearly need a simple online food ordering system that connects student with hut 
staff, let them order and pay online, and also provide delivery within the campus. 
**1.5 Scope:** 
The Online Food Ordering System for QAU Huts is designed to provide a digital 
platform that enables students, faculty members, and university employees at Quaid-i
Azam University Islamabad to order food from campus huts through web and mobile 
devices. 
The name of this project is Online Food Ordering System for huts at quaid e azam 
university islamabad. 
The main goal of our system is to allow students, faculty and university staff at QAU 
order food from huts using their phone or computer without going there. The system will 
handle everything from looking at the menu, placing order, making payment and 
tracking delivery. 
The system allows users to register and log in, browse food menus, search and filter 
items, place food orders, select delivery or pickup options, make online or cash 
payments, track order status in real time, cancel eligible orders, and submit feedback 
and ratings. 
The system also provides administrative features for managing food menus, processing 
customer orders, assigning delivery staff, and monitoring overall operations. 
The scope of the system is limited to food ordering and delivery services within the 
Quaid-i-Azam University campus. Delivery requests outside the university boundaries 
are not supported. The system aims to improve convenience, reduce waiting times, 
minimize overcrowding at university huts, and enhance the overall food service 
experience for students and staff.
