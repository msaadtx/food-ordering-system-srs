1. **Introduction** 
**1.1 BACKGROUND** 
Quaid-i-Azam University (QAU) is a big public university which is located in islamabad, 
pakistan. it have thousands of students, teachers and staff members who all live or 
study on this large campus. Because so many people are here, the university made 
small food places called huts on different areas of the campus so students can get food 
easily. 
Right now students have to go physically to these huts if they want food. there is a 
digital app but it have alot of problems and most students dont use it. This whole 
manual system cause many problems like long queues, no seats, bad weather outside 
and some huts are really far from certain departments so it take alot of time just to get 
food. 
This project is made by our group as part of our semester work at QAU. The main idea 
is to build a Online Food Ordering system so students can order food from their phone 
or laptop without actually going to the hut. 
**1.2  Existing systems** 
At QAU there is already one food ordering app but  it is not working good based on the 
student observation and informal  feedback, the existing system application is facing 
some serious issues like usability issues and reliability issues. So mostly students just 
go to hut themselfs, wait in line, tell the person what they want, and then carry food 
back themselves. 
These are the main features of the current system right now: 
• most orders are placed by going to hut in person becuase the app is not user 
friendly. 
• payment is always cash only, no online option. 
• no order tracking, students have no idea when food will be ready. 
• Menu is displayed on a physical board. 
• there is no delivery, student have to pick food themselves. 
• hut staff do everything by hand, orders, payments and all records are done 
manually. 
This system works fine when less students are there but when lunchtime or dinner time 
come and alot of students come at once it become very slow and messed up. 
**1.3 Issues in Existing Systems:** 
The current way of ordering food at QAU has alot of problems which effect both 
students and hut staff: 
• Long Waiting time: students has to stand in queues for long time at huts which 
waste there time specially between class timings. 
• Limited Seating: huts dont have enough seats. when many student come 
together most of them cant find any place to sit. 
• weather Problems: islamabad weather is very harsh. its very hot in summers, 
cold in winters and heavy rain also comes. students dont want to walk to huts in 
this type of weather. 
• Long Distance: some departments are very far from huts. students from those 
departments waste alot of time and energy just to go and get food. 
• Less Staff: huts dont have enough workers. when rush time comes they cant 
handle all orders at same time and everything become slow. 
• No Order Tracking: after placing order student have no way to know when it will 
be ready. they keep standing or have to come back again and again to check. 
• No Digital Records: hut management does not have any proper system. they cant 
keep track of sales, menu changes or order history.
